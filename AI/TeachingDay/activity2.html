Activity 2
Design better student practice

Goal

Explore ways in which students can use an LLM for active practice rather than as an answer machine.

Suggested task

  1. Pick a topic where students often need repetitive practice.
  2. Ask the LLM to act as a tutor that asks questions one at a time.
  3. Require hints before full solutions.
  4. Discuss whether this promotes understanding or dependency.

Example prompt

Act as a patient tutor for linear algebra. I am learning Gaussian elimination. Give me one matrix exercise at a time. Do not give the full solution immediately. First ask me for the next row operation, then respond with either a short hint or confirmation.

Questions for the group

  • Does the interaction encourage the student to think?
  • Can the prompt be improved to avoid oversharing?
  • Would you recommend this to students, and with what warnings?
  • How could this fit into office hours, support sessions, or homework advice?
