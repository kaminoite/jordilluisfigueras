AI in Teaching
Workshop for small-group discussion

Welcome

This website is the working space for the Teaching Day on Friday, 2026-03-26. The idea is simple: scan, browse, split into small groups, and discuss concrete uses of AI tools in teaching and learning.

How to use this site

  1. Open the overview page and read the short motivation.
  2. Choose one activity in a small group.
  3. Test a few prompts with an LLM interface available to you.
  4. Compare what worked, what failed, and what still needed human judgement.
  5. Report back with one useful use case, one limitation, and one concrete caution.
